About Google Gemini and more

Questions

Question not answered? Email info@ice-crystals.nl.

Do I need an API key?

Only for importing, translating and illustrations. You make a free key in Google AI Studio in a minute; the app shows you how. Typing recipes, cooking and sharing with your household work without a key.

What is sent to Google?

Only what you import, translate or have drawn: the link, the recipe text or your photo. Everything else stays on your phone.

Can someone in my household use my key?

Yes. Paired phones without a key of their own can use yours. You can turn that off in Settings.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not for now. Recipe Book is for Android; an iPhone version may come in the future.

Closed test

Try it before everyone else

Before Recipe Book goes on Google Play, a small group tests it for two weeks. You need an Android phone and a Google account.

  1. Join our Google Group with the Google account you use on your phone.
  2. Then open the test page on Google Play and tap “Become a tester”.
  3. Install the app, keep it for 14 days and use it now and then. Send feedback from the app.
