Changelog

What’s new

Every version of Recipe Book, newest first.

0.10.2 Cooking with ticks

Nov 10, 2026 Latest

  • New: Tick off steps while you cook. The app remembers where you were.
  • New: Fetch a recipe again from its source and have it rewritten in the house style.
  • Better: Steps show ingredient names and lengths such as 3 cm more clearly.

0.9.4 Ready for Google Play

Nov 10, 2026

  • New: The app is now called Recipe Book.
  • New: Send feedback or report an AI recipe that’s wrong, right from the app.
  • Better: The privacy statement is also online, in four languages.

0.8.0 A warm welcome

Nov 9, 2026

  • New: A short introduction on first start, with help getting your Gemini key.

0.7.3 Drawings for your recipes

Nov 9, 2026

  • New: No photo? Gemini draws a simple illustration.
  • New: Share your Gemini key with the phones in your household.
  • Better: Drag steps in the editor to reorder them.
  • Better: A privacy statement in the app.

0.6.0 All in one style

Nov 9, 2026

  • Better: Every question and message appears in the app’s own style.

0.5.3 Ratings

Nov 9, 2026

  • New: Rate recipes and note what to do differently next time.
  • New: Sort the overview by rating.
  • Better: Ratings are shared with your household and kept in backups.

0.4.3 Together with your household

Nov 9, 2026

  • New: Pair phones with a QR code and stay in sync over Wi-Fi.
  • New: Choose which Gemini model to use.
  • Fixed: Back always takes you to the overview.
  • Fixed: The logo on the dark splash screen is cut out cleanly.

0.3.3 Never lose a thing

Nov 9, 2026

  • New: A trash for deleted recipes, with restore.
  • New: Choose between merging and replacing when you restore a backup.
  • Better: Imports are faster and carry on in the background.

0.2.6 A face of its own

Nov 8, 2026

  • New: New logo and splash screen.
  • New: Choose light, dark or automatic.
  • Fixed: Recipe sites that refused the app now work.
  • Fixed: Newer Gemini keys are recognised.

0.1.0 The first version

Nov 8, 2026

  • New: Import recipes from a link, a photo or straight from your browser.
  • New: Change servings, metric or US units, with the screen on while you cook.
  • New: Search, categories and an A–Z bar.
  • New: Translate recipes and make backups.
